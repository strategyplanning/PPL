Key Uncertainty Factors  |  Informs: D1 – D7

The 12 drivers that determine which decision is right

These are not background factors — they are the variables that flip decisions. A shift in WTI from $75 to $120 changes the right answer on D1 (Cedar expansion), D2 (capital allocation), and D3 (pipeline sanctioning). A carbon price move from $80 to $200 reverses the right call on D4 (transition positioning). Track these to know when to revisit a decision.

WTI Crude Oil Price

Range: $45-150/bbl

Drives producer economics and activity levels. Polymarket: 65% odds WTI hits $120 in 2026. Pembina's fee-based model limits direct exposure but volumes correlate with producer health.

AECO Natural Gas Price

Range: C$1.50-7.00/GJ

Critical for Montney/Duvernay economics. LNG exports will structurally lift AECO. Cedar LNG adds 1.5 mtpa demand from late 2028. Pembina's gas processing is directly exposed.

WCSB Production Growth

Range: -3% to +8%/yr

WCSB grew 5.6% in 2024, 7% in 2025. Pipelines approaching capacity by Q1 2026. Pembina's volumes directly tied to basin activity. Record 3.7M boe/d in 2025.

Pipeline Egress Capacity

Range: Constrained to Surplus

TMX operational. Enbridge Mainline expansion (+150 kbpd) by 2027. If demand exceeds egress, Pembina's tolling power increases. Capacity = pricing power.

LNG Export Buildout

Range: Cedar only to 4+ projects

Cedar LNG (late 2028), LNG Canada Ph2 (FID 2026?), Woodfibre (2027-28), Ksi Lisims (pre-FID). Each project adds gas processing and pipeline demand for Pembina.

Canadian Carbon Policy

Range: C$80-250/tonne

Consumer tax removed. Industrial OBPS remains. 2026 federal review. Carney maintaining industrial carbon price. Higher prices = higher opex for Pembina but may slow producer activity.

Interest Rates / Debt Cost

Range: 3.5-6.0%

Pembina leverage target 3.5-4.25x. Cedar LNG is largest capex year in 2026 (~$1.6B total program). Leverage expected to return to low end by 2027 as Cedar spending ramps down.

Indigenous Partnerships

Range: Enabling to Blocking

Cedar LNG is a First Nations-led project (Haisla Nation). Indigenous partnerships increasingly critical for permitting and social license. Competitive advantage if managed well.

Energy Transition Pace

Range: Slow to Accelerated

Global pace of electrification, EV adoption, and renewable deployment determines long-term hydrocarbon demand. Pembina's 30+ year asset life means transition risk is real for terminal value.

Geopolitical Risk

Range: Stable to Crisis

Iran/Hormuz conflict driving oil above $100. Canadian energy sovereignty narrative strengthening. Trade tensions with US (tariffs on Canadian energy exports?) are an emerging risk.

Industry Consolidation

Range: Status quo to M&A wave

Midstream M&A active globally. Pembina could be acquirer (gas processing bolt-ons) or target (infrastructure funds, pension funds). Scale matters for LNG integration.

Hydrogen / CCUS Optionality

Range: No investment to Major pivot

Pembina's pipeline corridors could transport hydrogen or CO2. Alberta CCUS hubs emerging. Early-mover advantage possible but technology risk and policy dependence remain high.

Sources: Pembina Q4 2025 Earnings Release and 2026 Guidance; Pembina 2025 Annual Report; Polymarket oil predictions (March 31, 2026); CER Provincial Energy Profiles; Norton Rose Fulbright Canadian LNG Outlook 2026; Carbon Direct / Torys LLP Canadian carbon policy analysis; East Daley Canadian production forecasts.
